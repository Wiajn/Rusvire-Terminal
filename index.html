Standing Orders

📻 Radio Protocol
  • Radio check when occupying any position — at least one other SL must respond
  • No reception = no QRF capability. Move positions if no signal
  • One comms officer per squad — cannot be the Squad Leader
🎯 Hit Rules
  • Direct round hits only — ricochets don't count. Call "HIT" loudly and raise weapon and hand
  • 5-minute bleed-out timer. Can be revived with bandage (max 2 total)
  • After 2nd hit: requires combat medic syringe — none present for Rusvire this op, dice roll determines fate (even = alive, odd = dead)
  • Respawn: 150 paces then sit 30 seconds. Call "Back in!" loudly
  • If patient is hit while medic is touching them — medic is also hit
👁 Friendly ID & Camouflage
  • Blue tape on both biceps — apply before entering the field
  • Cannot identify target? Check fire. Confirm with battle buddy, unit sniper, or scoped squad member
  • Best camo: Multicam or Olive. Good: Tan. Avoid: black, pink, red, white, yellow, bright greens
  • Ghillies welcome. Saronde Valley is rocky ridges, open areas, and eucalyptus forest
💧 Logistics
  • Minimum 1.5L water per operator — recommended 3L
  • Ammo check after every contact. Drop spare ammo at base — be aware it can be looted
  • Current strength: 23 comrades
  • Inexperienced rental players placed under Alpha 1 / Bravo supervision or base protection
🔒 Capture Protocol
  • No capturing under-18s. No females over 18 without explicit consent. No medical conditions without consent
  • Say "you are my captive" and start 30-minute timer. No timing device = invalid capture
  • Game-related questions only. Captive keeps their phone and radio
  • Release after 30 minutes — captive takes 150 paces then is fully respawned
Simple rule for everyone: If you're ever unsure what to do — find Alpha 1 or Bravo 1, stick to them, and ask.
